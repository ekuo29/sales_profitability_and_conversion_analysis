The source workbook carries IIMB copyright, and both Office files retain author metadata. Keep these copies local unless the rights holder and course instructor explicitly authorize uploading them.

The filenames and hashes in `../CHECKSUMS.sha256` can be used to confirm that the local copies have not changed.
